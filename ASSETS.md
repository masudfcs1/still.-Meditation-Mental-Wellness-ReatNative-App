# Still artwork and sound

Photography is bundled locally so sessions do not request third-party images at runtime.

- Forest: Unsplash image `photo-1448375240586-882707db888b`
- Mountain: Unsplash image `photo-1464822759023-fed622ff2c3b`
- Ocean: Unsplash image `photo-1518837695005-2083093ee35b`
- Desert: Unsplash image `photo-1509316785289-025f5b846b35`
- Lake: Unsplash image `photo-1470770841072-f978cf4d019e`
- Night sky: Unsplash image `photo-1475274047050-1d0c0975c63e`

Source images are available through `https://images.unsplash.com/<image-id>`. Photography is used under the [Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license).

The six ambient WAV files are original procedural sound textures generated by `scripts/generate_audio.py` (Python + NumPy). They contain no third-party recordings or spoken narration. Regeneration is optional; the audio is already bundled.

The leaf brand mark is original vector geometry, rendered into app icons by `scripts/generate_brand.py` (Python + Pillow). Icons use Lucide. Manrope and Lora font files are bundled through their Expo Google Fonts packages and distributed under their included open font licenses.
